Hi, I'm Plink. I listen while you play.

The piano practice tracker that logs itself.

Connect your digital piano and play. Plink keeps your streak, finds the pieces you're learning and shows how they're getting better. Nothing to start, nothing to type.

It starts when you play

Plug your piano in over USB or pair it over Bluetooth MIDI. Every session is saved when you stop. No record button.

★

A streak that's kind

A daily goal you pick, a ring that fills as you play, and a week that shows the days that counted.

♫

Pieces, found from the notes

No sheet music needed. Plink groups what you play into pieces and tracks clean plays, stumbles and tempo for each.

▶

Hear it back

Replay any session with a real piano sound and see the notes fall, to hear how far you've come.

⇄

iPhone and iPad

Practice on either one. Turn on iCloud sync and your history follows you, in your own iCloud.

✓

No account, ever

Plink works offline from the first minute. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.

Works with your digital piano

Plink listens over USB or Bluetooth MIDI, which most digital pianos have, including the Roland FP, Yamaha P and Kawai ES series. Plug in a USB cable, or pair over Bluetooth once. Tested on the Roland FP-30X.

Your practice stays yours.

Plink has no servers. What you play stays on your devices, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on sync. We never see it. Read the privacy policy.

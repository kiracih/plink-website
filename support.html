Connecting your piano

  • USB: connect the piano's USB port (the square "to host" port) to your iPhone or iPad with a cable or Apple's camera adapter, then play. Plink records while it's open on screen.
  • Bluetooth: in Plink, open Connect › Bluetooth and pair the piano there, not in iOS Settings. Over Bluetooth Plink keeps recording with the screen locked.
  • A piano's Bluetooth connects to one phone or tablet at a time. Apps on that device can share it, so Plink works alongside your piano maker's app.
  • Playing an acoustic piano? Use the practice timer on Today, or add past practice.

Plink Plus

  • Recording, streaks, goals, reminders, the last 30 days and iCloud sync are free.
  • Plink Plus adds your full history, piece details, the year view, the 3D keys and every challenge.
  • Restore a purchase: Settings › Plink Plus › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel or change your plan: iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • There's no account. Your practice stays on your device and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud.
  • To move to a new phone, turn on iCloud sync, or export your data in Settings and import the file on the new device.
  • Details are in the privacy policy.

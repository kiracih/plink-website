Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

In short

  • Plink has no account, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your practice is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also stored in your own private iCloud, which only you can read.
  • We, the makers of Plink, never receive your practice data.

Who we are

Plink Piano ("Plink", "we") is an app made by an independent developer in Istanbul, Türkiye. This policy covers the Plink app for iPhone and iPad and this website.

What Plink records

When your digital piano is connected, Plink records what the piano sends over MIDI: which keys you press, when, how hard, and your pedals. From that it works out your practice time, streak, pieces and progress. Plink also keeps the settings you choose, such as your daily goal and the names you give your pieces.

Plink does not use your microphone or camera, does not collect your name, email, contacts or location, and does not ask you to sign in.

Where it is stored

  • On your device. Everything Plink records is stored in the app on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • In your own iCloud, if you turn on sync. Plink can sync your history between your devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in your private database. Apple stores it under your Apple Account. We can't access it. You can turn sync off in Plink's Settings and manage iCloud data in your device's Settings. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Files you share. If you export a session (for example as a MIDI file or video), it goes only where you send it.

Notifications

If you allow them, Plink's practice reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server, and nothing about your practice leaves your device to create them.

Bluetooth

Plink uses Bluetooth only to connect to your piano or MIDI adapter, when you choose Bluetooth.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active subscription.

Diagnostics

Plink includes no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. If you choose in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage reports with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This site is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. It sets no cookies and has no analytics of ours. GitHub and Google may log technical data such as your IP address when serving files. See GitHub's and Google's privacy policies.

Children

Plink collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it can be used by learners of any age.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, you control it directly: you can view it in the app, export it, and delete it by deleting sessions, turning off sync and deleting the app. If you have a question about your data, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what Plink collects, we'll say so in the app before it applies.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on this site's repository.

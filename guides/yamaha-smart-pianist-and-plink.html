Yamaha Smart Pianist vs Plink: can you use both?

Last updated 12 November 2026

Short answer: yes. They do different jobs. Smart Pianist is Yamaha's free app for its digital pianos: it changes voices and settings, shows scores for up to 400 songs, makes chord charts and records a take when you press record. Plink logs every practice session by itself, on any digital piano: your time, streaks, the pieces you play and the spots you stumble on, with nothing to open or start. A piano's Bluetooth MIDI usually connects to one phone or tablet at a time. On that device, iOS shares the connection between apps, so both can run together, or you can give each device its own connection: Bluetooth for one, the USB cable for the other.

How to connect both

P-525 (built-in Bluetooth MIDI):

  1. Connect the piano's USB TO HOST port to your iPhone or iPad with a cable. Plink picks it up straight away.
  2. Connect Smart Pianist over Bluetooth. Yamaha's manual says to connect Bluetooth Audio first, then Bluetooth MIDI.

P-225 and models without built-in Bluetooth MIDI: Smart Pianist needs the USB cable or Yamaha's UD-BT01 adaptor. Without the adaptor, if both apps are on the same iPhone they can share the cable; if they're on two devices, use one at a time: open Smart Pianist to change settings, then close it and play with Plink.

Good to know: over Bluetooth, Smart Pianist's audio recording doesn't work (Yamaha says so in its connection guide). Plink records MIDI, so this doesn't affect your practice log.

If Plink doesn't see the piano

  • Close Smart Pianist and any other music app, then open Plink again.
  • Use the USB cable. It always works.

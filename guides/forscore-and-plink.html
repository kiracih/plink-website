Using forScore with a digital piano and a practice tracker

Last updated 12 November 2026

Short answer: yes, you can use both. Most digital pianos accept one Bluetooth MIDI device at a time, and forScore on an iPad can take it for page turns. (Apps on the same device share the connection; it's a second device that can't join.) So connect the practice tracker with a USB cable and leave Bluetooth to forScore, or the other way round. forScore counts the time a score is open; Plink counts the time you actually play.

Set-up that works

  1. iPhone with Plink: connect it to the piano's USB port with a cable. Plink starts logging from the first note.
  2. iPad with forScore on the music stand: in forScore, open Settings → MIDI → Bluetooth MIDI devices and connect the piano if you want MIDI page turns. Otherwise use a Bluetooth page-turn pedal, which doesn't use the piano's MIDI link.
  3. Only Bluetooth, no cable? Pick one device for the piano's Bluetooth MIDI, or run forScore and Plink on the same iPad. Turn pages with a pedal or by tapping in forScore, and let Plink have the piano.

If an app can't find the piano

  • Disconnect the piano in the other app (in forScore: Settings → MIDI → Bluetooth MIDI devices), then try again.
  • Use a USB cable. It always works.

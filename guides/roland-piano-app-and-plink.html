Roland Piano App vs Plink: can you use both?

Last updated 9 November 2026

Short answer: yes. They do different jobs. The Roland Piano App is a remote control and lesson app for Roland pianos: it changes settings, shows scores, runs flash cards and records a take when you ask it to. Plink logs every practice session by itself, on any digital piano: your time, streaks, the pieces you play and the spots you stumble on, with nothing to open or start. Run Plink over USB and the Roland app over Bluetooth, because Bluetooth MIDI connects to one app at a time.

How to use both on a Roland FP-30X

  1. Connect the FP-30X to your iPhone or iPad with a USB cable (USB-B on the piano to USB-C). Plink picks it up straight away.
  2. Pair the Roland Piano App over Bluetooth for settings and lessons.
  3. Only use Bluetooth? Then use one app at a time: close the Roland app before opening Plink, or the other way round.

How to connect a Roland FP-30X to an iPhone or iPad

Last updated 10 November 2026

Short answer: use a USB cable (USB-B on the piano to USB-C or Lightning on the phone) for the most reliable connection. Or use Bluetooth MIDI, which you pair inside the app, not in iOS Settings. The piano's Function button lights blue when Bluetooth is connected.

USB cableBluetooth MIDI
What you needUSB-B to USB-C cable (iPhone 15 and later, recent iPads) or Apple's Lightning to USB Camera AdapterNothing extra
Where you pairNothing to pair: plug it inInside the app
ReliabilityBestGood. One app connection at a time

Option 1: USB cable (recommended)

  1. Connect the USB Computer port on the back of the FP-30X to your iPhone or iPad. Use a USB-B to USB-C cable, or a USB-B cable plus Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter.
  2. Open your app. The piano appears as a MIDI device straight away.

Option 2: Bluetooth MIDI

  1. Turn on Bluetooth on the iPhone. If the piano's MIDI entry (such as FP-30X MIDI) shows up in Settings → Bluetooth, don't tap it there. Bluetooth MIDI is paired inside apps.
  2. Open your practice app and use its Bluetooth or MIDI connection screen. In Plink, that's Bluetooth on the first run, or Settings → Pair a Bluetooth piano.
  3. Choose the piano and accept the pairing request. The piano's Function button lights blue.
  4. If the piano doesn't appear, hold [Function] and press the lowest key (A0) to turn the piano's Bluetooth on, then try again.

Bluetooth audio (for playing phone music through the piano's speakers) is a separate link. You pair it in iOS Settings by holding [Function] for five seconds. You don't need it for practice apps.

Source for the button steps: Roland, How to connect your Roland FP-30X to Bluetooth and Apps.

If it won't connect

  • Close other music apps. Bluetooth MIDI connects to one app at a time.
  • Forget the piano in iOS Settings → Bluetooth, switch Bluetooth off and on, and pair again from the app.
  • Restart the piano and the phone. Update iOS and the app.
  • Still stuck? Use the USB cable. It always works.

What can you do once it's connected?

Lesson apps and score apps read the keys you play. Plink keeps a practice log by itself: plug in or pair once, then just play. It records your practice time, streaks and the pieces you play, with no timer to start. Plink is our app, and it's coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Kawai PianoRemote vs Plink: can you use both?

Last updated 12 November 2026

Short answer: yes. They do different jobs. PianoRemote is Kawai's free remote control app: it changes sounds and settings, runs the Lesson and Concert Magic functions and the metronome, and records a take when you set it up. Plink logs every practice session by itself, on any digital piano: your time, streaks, the pieces you play and the spots you stumble on, with nothing to open or start. Kawai says: "Most Kawai pianos support only one Bluetooth MIDI connection at a time." That limit is per phone or tablet: on one iPhone, iOS shares the connection between apps, as we saw with Roland's app and Plink on an FP-30X.

Sources (read 11 November 2026): PianoRemote on the App Store; Kawai's FAQs on recording and on connecting several devices (kawaius.com). Plink is coming soon to the App Store.

How to use both

  1. Connect the piano's USB port to your iPhone or iPad with a cable. Plink picks it up straight away.
  2. Pair PianoRemote over Bluetooth MIDI for sounds, settings and lessons.
  3. Two devices, such as an iPad for PianoRemote and an iPhone for Plink? Only one can use the piano's Bluetooth, so give the other the USB cable. To switch devices, Kawai advises: "unpair the current device and pair the new one."

Kawai's score app PiaBookPlayer also connects over Bluetooth MIDI, so the same one-device rule applies.

If Plink doesn't see the piano

  • Close PianoRemote and PiaBookPlayer, then open Plink again.
  • Use the USB cable. It always works.
